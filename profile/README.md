### What is DevLyft?

Most teams don't need more cloud — they need less of it to think about. Picking a region, sizing a node, watching an autoscaler at 2am: none of that is the product you're building.

**DevLyft** is one platform that handles region placement, isolation, and scaling for you — automatically. You supply the artifact; we keep it running, wherever it needs to run, for exactly as long as it's needed.

### Why DevLyft

- **Ship in seconds** — push a container, get a running deployment.
- **Shared or dedicated nodes** — choose the isolation you need, per workload.
- **Regions you choose** — run close to your users.
- **Per-second billing** — pay for the CPU and memory you request, with no platform fee.
- **No clusters to operate** — no YAML sprawl, no pager.

### Built for

Solo developers shipping a side project, small teams without a dedicated ops person, startups scaling from zero to real traffic, agencies hosting projects for multiple clients, open-source maintainers running demos & docs, and students learning to ship real infrastructure.
